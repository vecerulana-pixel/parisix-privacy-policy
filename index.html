PARI HERO / WHERE YOU ARE LTD

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Your practice records stay on your device.

Pari Hero is a volleyball serve-practice app published by WHERE YOU ARE LTD. The native app does not require an account and does not upload your practice records.

Information stored by the app

Pari Hero stores the information you enter to support its practice features:

  • Block names, serve styles, target zones and planned repetition counts.
  • Recorded serve landings, net and out results, and block creation and completion dates.
  • Your reflections, onboarding choices and appearance preference.

These records are saved in the app's private local storage. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Firebase Realtime Database

The app includes Firebase Realtime Database, a Google service, configured for the app's Firebase project. In the current native version, the practice features do not read or write your blocks, serve entries or reflections to Firebase. These features remain usable offline. The Firebase SDK includes network access needed for database connectivity.

Firebase Analytics is not used. The app does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics trackers or account sign-in.

This policy page

This static page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it connects to GitHub's hosting infrastructure, which may process ordinary connection information such as your IP address and request details to serve the page. WHERE YOU ARE LTD does not add tracking scripts, analytics, cookies or forms to this page.

Device permissions

Pari Hero does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or shared-file access for its native practice features. Network access supports the configured SDK and loading this policy.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can edit a setup before recording serves, correct the latest serve, update reflections and delete individual blocks. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app's local records. We do not hold a server copy of your practice history.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Please include only the information needed to explain your request. Do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
